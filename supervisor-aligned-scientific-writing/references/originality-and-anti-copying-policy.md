# Originality and Anti-Copying Policy (final)

## 1. Core qualitative originality rule
Do not intentionally reproduce distinctive wording or closely paraphrase
reference-corpus sources. Perform a qualitative originality review unless
an authorized similarity-analysis tool is available and has actually been
used. Do not claim measured similarity, plagiarism scores, or a
plagiarism-pass result without such a tool.

## 2. What may be borrowed (structure, not text)
- Rhetorical moves and section architectures (context → gap → … as default).
- Genre conventions (IMRaD order, abstract move sequence, point-by-point
  reviewer replies).
- Generic scientific connectors and standard transitions.

## 3. What must never be borrowed from the reference corpus
- Distinctive wording, memorable phrasings, rhetorical "signatures".
- Data, numerical results, methods specifics, citations, hypotheses,
  scientific ideas, conclusions.
- The corpus's examples, figures, tables.

## 4. Composition rule
Every sentence is composed fresh for the current task's content. The corpus
informs *how* to structure an argument — never *which words* to use.

## 5. Paraphrase distance
If an idea must be traceable to a source: either (a) express it in fully new
sentence architecture and vocabulary, and cite it, or (b) quote it
explicitly with citation (rare; only for definitions or official standards).

## 6. Qualitative originality review (before delivering any draft)
- Re-read the draft against the reference-corpus sources: no mirrored
  sentences, no recycled distinctive phrases.
- Any near-match: restructure the sentence completely or delete it.
- Verify no corpus datum, method parameter, or citation leaked into the
  draft.
- Report the review as qualitative — no claim of measured similarity,
  plagiarism scores, or a plagiarism-pass result unless an authorized
  similarity-analysis tool is available and has actually been used on the
  draft.

## 7. Similarity discipline
- No "collage" text (stitched fragments from sources).
- One draft, one authorial voice; consistent register across sections.
- When editing user-supplied text: preserve meaning, rewrite wording; flag
  any passage too close to a supplied reference.

## 8. Provenance of the rules themselves
The style rules in this skill derive from high-level, non-substitutable
patterns only; they contain no corpus sentences. Any future re-examination
of corpus files is read-only and for pattern verification only.
