# Persian Proposal-Writing Rules (fa, revised)

For INSF-style and similar funder proposals. Tags: [S] supervisor pattern /
[J] Persian genre convention / [I] improvement.

## Macro-architecture (default section order)
[S/J] Follow the funder's official template when provided. Default
INSF-style order:
1. مطابقت طرح با اولویت‌های اعلامشده (alignment with announced priorities)
2. بیان مسئله و شکاف پژوهشی (problem statement & research gap)
3. ضرورت و اهمیت اجرای طرح (necessity & importance)
4. مرور پیشینه تحقیق و بررسی پتنت‌ها (literature & patent analysis)
5. اهداف اصلی و اختصاصی (general & specific objectives)
6. فرضیات و سؤالات تحقیق (hypotheses & research questions)
7. زمینه استفاده و کاربرد نتایج (applications of results)
8. روش انجام طرح (work plan — numbered protocol subsections)
9. روش‌های پردازش و تحلیل داده‌ها (computational/statistical methods)
10. دستاوردهای کاربردی و افق آینده (deliverables & outlook)
11. تجهیزات، مواد و نیروی انسانی (resources — existing vs. required)
12. فواید اقتصادی و ملی (economic & national benefits)
13. مدیریت ریسک (risk management)
14. تعهد اخلاقی و عدم هم‌پوشانی علمی (ethics & originality declaration)
15. فهرست منابع و مآخذ (references)

## Operational rules
- [S] Quantitative/economic problem framing belongs here: magnitudes,
  percentages, concrete cost examples, national statistics. Every number
  needs a verifiable source or `[نیازمند منبع]`.
- [S] Bullet lists for hypotheses, necessity dimensions, risks, deliverables.
- [S] Executive first-person-plural voice for objectives and work plan;
  formal register elsewhere.
- [S] Explicit alignment with the funder's priority headings (ستادها),
  with policy/technology citations.
- [S] Risk section: name each risk, then its mitigation, paired.
- [S] Ethics: state scope (e.g., no human/animal/plant intervention if so);
  data-safety commitments; originality declaration.
- [I] Keep hypotheses testable and separated from justifications; mark
  non-testable assumptions as فرض مبنایی (framing assumption), not فرضیه.
- [I] Resources section: clearly separate موجود (available) from
  مورد نیاز (required).
- [I] Long compound sentences are acceptable in Persian; still keep the main
  verb early enough that the sentence stays followable.
- [J] Correct use of نیمفاصله (ZWNJ) for می- prefixes and -ها suffixes;
  standard Persian scientific equivalents with the Latin term in parentheses
  on first use.
- [I] National-context themes (تحریم، بومی‌سازی، حاکمیت داده) are
  genre-appropriate here — and must NOT be carried into English manuscripts.

## Gap markers in Persian outputs (mandatory)
- `[نیازمند منبع]` — جای خالی مرجع
- `[نیازمند داده]` — جای خالی داده/پشتوانهٔ عددی
- `[نیازمند جزئیات روش]` — جای خالی پارامتر روشی
- `[نیازمند تأیید نویسنده]` — مورد نیازمند تأیید نویسنده

## Persian → English transfer (for translation tasks)
- [S] Reconstruct; never translate literally. Transfer the idea-unit into
  natural English sentence architecture.
- [I] Split one long Persian sentence into 2–3 English sentences; convert
  Persian passive stacks into natural English passive voice or nominalization.
- [I] Neutralize or drop institutional/political Persian phrasing per target
  venue norms; never translate word-for-word.
- [I] Numbers, units, names, and citations transfer exactly; flag any
  non-standard term with `[author confirmation needed]` (English output
  uses the English marker set).
