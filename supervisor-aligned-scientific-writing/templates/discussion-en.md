# Discussion Skeleton (EN)

Mirror the Results' subsection order. Per finding:
1. **Our result** — [restated compactly]
2. **Literature comparison** — [consistent / in contrast; else [citation needed]]
3. **Explanation** — [mechanism or practice; hypotheses marked as hypotheses]

Then:
- **Limitations** — [explicit passage; boundary of validity]
- **Prospective outlook** — [clearly prospective; no claim of achievement]

Claim calibration: direct for strong data; hedged for partial; no causal
verbs without causal design.
